Who I Am

I’m a developer, data engineer, and problem-solver with a passion for creating smart, scalable tech solutions. I thrive at the intersection of code, creativity, and curiosity.

Armed with a strong foundation in Data Analytics and Computer Science, I specialize in building systems that not only function — but perform, adapt, and scale.

I’m currently pursuing my Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Christ University, Bangalore. Before this, I worked as an ML Engineer at CVC Networks, where I helped design and researching in many ML-related areas.

With a Bachelor's degree in Data Analytics, I’ve developed an edge in blending development with insight — understanding not just how to build, but what to build, and why it matters.

I specialize in:

  • Full-stack development (React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL)
  • Data engineering (ETL pipelines, data wrangling, analytics dashboards)
  • AI/ML exploration, with a strong foundation in Python and practical experience deploying models
  • DevOps with Docker and Jenkins for streamlined CI/CD workflows

My work spans hackathons, academic research, open-source contributions, and real-world deployment. I don’t just chase trends — I build tools that solve problems.
